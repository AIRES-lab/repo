Artificial Intelligence Research for Experimental Security

AIRES Lab studies how artificial intelligence can strengthen security research through generative agents, computational experimentation, and rigorous socio-technical analysis.

About AIRES Get in Touch

Lab focus

Empowering security studies with artificial intelligence

AIRES Lab develops and evaluates AI-enabled methods for studying security problems where technical systems, institutions, information environments, and human behavior interact. The lab is especially interested in generative agents, experimental simulation, computational social science, and responsible AI use in security research.

Subprojects

Current directions

Synthetic Samples for Security Research

Privacy-preserving synthetic samples and scenario materials for testing research designs, training analytical workflows, and studying sensitive security problems without exposing operational data.

Generative Agents in Security Studies

Conceptual and experimental work on how generative agents can support security studies by modeling strategic behavior, institutional settings, information environments, and escalation dynamics.

Large Security Datasets and Computational Conflict Research

Work with large-scale datasets on conflict, cybersecurity, regimes, and political risk to support reproducible security research and AI-assisted empirical analysis.

European Research Consortium Building

Development of a European research network around AI-enabled security studies, responsible experimentation, governance, and socio-technical methods for future collaborative funding calls.
